[Scene_001.jpg] Welcome to an incredible discovery about the human brain.
Have you ever felt your phone vibrate, only to find nothing there?
[Scene_002.jpg] This phenomenon is called Phantom Vibration Syndrome.
Scientists first studied it in 2013 at a university in Illinois.
[Scene_003.jpg] Up to 89 percent of people experience this sensation.
It affects both men and women equally.
[Scene_004.jpg] Your brain is constantly on alert for notifications.
The anticipation creates false sensations in your pocket.
[Scene_005.jpg] This happens more often with heavy smartphone users.
People who check their phones over 50 times a day are most affected.
[Scene_006.jpg] It is completely normal and not a sign of dependency.
Simply knowing about it can reduce the anxiety it causes.
